PreFrame 隐私政策

生效及最近更新：2026 年 11 月 16 日。适用于全功能免费版 1.0（构建 6）及以后版本。

中文English
简要说明：PreFrame 不收集数据。拍摄、RAW 处理、相纸模拟、扫描、曝光校准和相册管理在设备本地完成。App 不提供反馈上传功能，不自动上传照片、扫描、DNG、拍摄参数或使用记录；无账号、广告或跨 App 跟踪。

1. 运营者与联系

PreFrame 由 Flashbrick Lab 提供。隐私问题请联系 flashbrick.lab@gmail.com。

2. 本机图像处理

相机画面、照片、RAW/DNG、曝光和镜头参数、扫描、配对、收藏及编辑内容均在设备上处理和保存，不发送到我们的服务器。本机曝光校准只用于你设备上的后续图像处理。

3. 本地保存与删除

成片、扫描及参数保留在设备上，你可以在 PreFrame 相册内主动删除。RAW 缓存按你选择的保留时间和容量上限自动清理；清理 RAW 不会删除对应成片和参数记录。卸载 App 会移除其本地数据。已导出至系统“照片”的副本需要在“照片”App 中单独管理。

4. 系统权限

  • 相机：用于取景、拍摄和实体相纸扫描。
  • 照片（仅添加）：在你主动保存或导出时写入结果。
  • 运动与深度能力：在受支持设备上辅助扫描稳定性、边框和透视计算，仅在本机使用。

你可以在 iOS“设置”中撤销权限。拒绝权限只会影响依赖该权限的功能。

5. 免费使用与分享

全部功能免费，无订阅或内购，App 不读取 StoreKit 购买权益或处理支付资料。只有在你主动选择保存、分享或打开外部网页时，系统才会将所选内容或请求交由对应目标处理；这些目标按各自的隐私政策提供服务。

6. 支持邮件

如你主动通过支持邮箱联系我们，邮件由你选择的邮件服务发送，内容仅用于回复与处理你的请求。请勿发送不必要的私人照片或 RAW 文件。邮件不通过 App 自动提交。

7. 跟踪、儿童与更新

PreFrame 不出售个人信息，不使用广告标识符、第三方分析 SDK 或跨 App 跟踪，也不要求注册账号。App 面向一般摄影用户，不以儿童为目标。数据处理方式改变时，我们会更新本政策和日期。

English version

PreFrame Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 16, 2026. Applies to the fully free app version 1.0 (build 6) and later.

Summary: PreFrame does not collect data. Capture, RAW processing, instant-print simulation, scanning, exposure calibration, and album management take place on your device. The app has no feedback-upload feature and does not automatically upload photos, scans, DNG files, capture parameters, or usage records. It has no accounts, ads, or cross-app tracking.

Operator and contact

PreFrame is provided by Flashbrick Lab. Contact flashbrick.lab@gmail.com for privacy questions.

On-device processing

Camera frames, photos, RAW/DNG files, exposure and lens parameters, scans, pairings, favorites, and edits are processed and stored locally. They are not sent to our servers. Local exposure calibration affects only image processing on your device.

Storage and deletion

Delete saved photos, scans, and parameter records in the PreFrame album. RAW caches are cleaned according to your selected retention period and size budget; RAW cleanup does not delete finished images or their metadata. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Manage exported copies separately in Apple Photos.

Permissions

Camera access supports capture and scanning. Add-only Photos access saves exports only when requested. Device motion and supported depth capabilities assist scan stability, edges, and perspective calculations locally. You can revoke permissions in iOS Settings.

Free access and sharing

All features are free, without subscriptions or in-app purchases. The app does not read StoreKit purchase entitlements or process payment information. Only when you choose to save, share, or open an external webpage does the system pass your selected content or request to that destination. Other destinations apply their own privacy policies.

Support email

If you contact support, your chosen mail service sends your message. We use it only to respond and handle your request. Do not send unnecessary private images or RAW files. The app does not automatically submit email.

Tracking, children, and changes

PreFrame does not sell personal information or use advertising identifiers, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking, and it does not require an account. It is a general photography tool, not directed at children. We will update this policy and its date if data practices change.
